2. 数据存储 / Data Storage

所有数据均存储在您的设备本地，App 不会将任何数据上传到服务器。使用 iOS 沙盒隔离保护确保数据安全。

  • ✅ 完全本地化运行
  • ✅ 无云端同步功能
  • ✅ 数据只保存在您的设备上（指 App 不收集、不上传数据；关于系统备份请见下方说明）

iOS 系统备份：如果您开启了 iCloud 备份或使用电脑进行系统备份（访达 / iTunes），本 App 的数据文件可能会包含在系统备份中，这是 iOS 的标准行为。您可以在 iOS"设置 > 您的姓名 > iCloud > 管理账户储存空间 > 备份"中自行关闭对本 App 的备份。

Widget 数据共享：桌面小组件（Widget）通过 iOS App Group 机制读取 App 提供的展示数据（如最新体重、连续记录天数）。这些数据仅保存在您的设备本地，不会上传到任何服务器。

All data is stored locally on your device. The app never uploads any data to a server. Data security is ensured through iOS sandbox isolation.

  • ✅ Fully local operation
  • ✅ No cloud sync functionality
  • ✅ Data stays on your device (meaning the app does not collect or upload data; see the note below about system backups)

iOS system backups: If you enable iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer (Finder / iTunes), this app's data files may be included in the system backup. This is standard iOS behavior. You can turn off backups for this app in iOS Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Backups.

Widget data sharing: The home-screen widget reads display data (such as your latest weight and logging streak) from the app via the iOS App Group mechanism. This data remains entirely on your device and is never uploaded to any server.
